=== ppt/slides/slide8.xml ===
The exercise section is designed to work like a vending machine, where the player must enter a code to request the item they need. There may be traps or useless objects, such as a toy, a harness, a ball, and other items. | This section will be similar to Roblox games, where players are asked to enter a specific code to unlock a door. However, instead of opening a door, the machine will dispense the requested item once the correct code is entered. | To make the exercise easier, each object can be represented by a number (1, 2, 3, etc.) instead of requiring the player to type the item's name. In this section, the player will learn about gravity and receive information explaining why exercising is essential in space. They will discover how the lack of physical activity in microgravity can lead to muscle loss, bone density reduction, and premature aging effects on the body. | When it comes to sleeping, the player will only need to decide where they want to sleep. In space, there is no concept of vertical or horizontal, so astronauts can simply attach their sleeping bags to the floor or walls. However, they must choose a location near ventilation grilles that remove the CO₂ we produce when breathing. If they fail to do so, they risk fatal carbon dioxide poisoning. | There is no need to create a highly detailed environment for this section. The main requirement is to include suitable ventilation grilles in different sleeping areas. | Once the player chooses where to sleep, if they select the correct location, we can include a short animation showing the character going to sleep in that spot while the ventilation grilles extract the CO₂ from the surrounding air. The player will no longer control the character during this sequence; the game will automatically guide them to the sleeping area. | This completes Level 1. | Level 1 should last a maximum of 7 to 8 minutes.
